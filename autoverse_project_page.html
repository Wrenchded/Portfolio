AutoVerse — AI-Powered Automotive Marketplace

A full-stack automotive marketplace enabling vehicle browsing, comparison, rentals, test-drive booking, and listing management, with ML-driven used-car price prediction and an AI-powered recommendation engine. Includes MobileNetV2-based vehicle damage detection and an immersive 3D virtual showroom for exploring every vehicle up close.

3D Virtual Showroom ML Price Prediction Damage Detection AI Smart Recommendations
Python FastAPI Streamlit PostgreSQL Scikit-learn TensorFlow MobileNetV2
01

The Explore Cars page serves as the primary browsing interface of the AutoVerse platform, allowing users to explore a curated collection of luxury automobiles through an intuitive and interactive dashboard. Vehicle information is dynamically loaded from the dataset and presented in visually appealing card-based layouts displaying images, specifications, pricing, fuel type, and descriptions. Users can refine their search using advanced filters such as brand, price range, and fuel type to quickly locate vehicles that match their preferences. Each car listing provides direct action buttons to Buy, Rent, or Book a Test Drive, enabling seamless navigation to the respective modules. Additionally, integrated promotional videos and responsive UI components enhance user engagement, providing a premium digital showroom experience for potential buyers.

02

The Test Drive Booking page enables users to conveniently schedule a test drive for their selected vehicle through a simple and user-friendly interface. After choosing a car from the Explore Cars page, the vehicle details are automatically displayed, allowing users to enter their personal information, including name, email address, phone number, and preferred date and time for the test drive. The system validates all user inputs before securely storing the booking information in the database. Upon successful submission, an automated confirmation email containing the booking details is sent to the customer's registered email address. This module streamlines the appointment scheduling process, enhances customer convenience, and improves communication between the dealership and prospective buyers.

03

The Rent Car page allows users to rent premium vehicles by selecting their preferred rental duration through an intuitive booking interface. The page displays detailed information about the selected car, including specifications, description, and estimated daily rental cost. Users are required to provide personal details, upload identity proof, enter their address, and digitally sign the rental agreement before confirming the booking. The system automatically calculates the total rental amount based on the selected pickup and return dates, validates the user's wallet balance, and records the rental details upon successful confirmation. A confirmation email is then sent to the registered email address, providing complete booking information. This module offers a secure, streamlined, and user-friendly vehicle rental experience while maintaining proper booking records and customer verification.

04

The Damage Detection page utilizes a Deep Learning–based image classification model to automatically identify whether a vehicle has sustained visible damage. Users can upload an image of a car through a simple drag-and-drop interface, after which the image is securely transmitted to the FastAPI backend for analysis. The backend processes the image using a pre-trained MobileNetV2 model and returns the prediction results, including the damage status, confidence score, and overall damage percentage. These results are displayed through an interactive dashboard featuring visual indicators and progress bars, allowing users to quickly assess the vehicle's condition. This AI-powered module assists buyers, sellers, and inspectors by providing fast, accurate, and automated vehicle damage assessment, thereby reducing manual inspection efforts and improving decision-making.

05

The AI Price Prediction page enables users to estimate the current market value of a vehicle using a Machine Learning–based prediction model. Users provide essential vehicle details such as current price, year of purchase, distance driven, fuel type, transmission type, seller type, and number of previous owners through an interactive form. These inputs are sent to the FastAPI backend, where a trained regression model processes the data and predicts the vehicle's estimated selling price. The predicted value is then displayed instantly on the interface, allowing users to make informed pricing decisions before buying or selling a vehicle. This intelligent module improves pricing accuracy, enhances transparency, and supports fair market valuation by leveraging historical automobile data and predictive analytics.

06

The AI Chatbot page provides users with an intelligent conversational interface for obtaining quick information about vehicles available on the AutoVerse platform. The chatbot processes natural language queries related to car brands, models, pricing, fuel type, performance, acceleration, top speed, mileage, and new or pre-owned vehicles. Based on the user's input, it searches the automobile dataset and returns relevant vehicle details in real time. The chatbot maintains the conversation history throughout the session, enabling a smooth and interactive user experience. By offering instant responses to common automobile-related queries, this module simplifies vehicle exploration and assists users in making informed decisions without manually searching through the catalog.
